Strategy

Objective

Make the duo a habit, not a haul.

Measured on saves and duo purchase rate rather than impressions. The goal is not just to get watched, but to make the pairing worth coming back to.

Insight

Effortless is aspirational but boring to watch.

This audience wants to look like they didn't try. The routine has to be short enough to copy and satisfying enough to watch all the way through, which makes pacing just as important as the message.

Big idea

Milk, then bronze.

Naming the order turns two products into one routine, and that routine becomes a repeatable content format. Every video follows the same two beats with a different face, setting, and light.

Pillars

Routine · Proof · Chorus · Reply

GRWMs build repetition, creator tutorials answer "does it work on me?", UGC and stitches extend the idea through the community, and comment replies become content of their own.

Design decisions

Type

One soft geometric sans, two weights.

Every frame runs the same rounded sans as the wordmark: the line set large and tight in deep brown, the step label small and light above it. I never mix in a second face — the only hierarchy is size and weight, so the type never competes with the product.

Color

One warm ramp, no second accent: cream and milk on one side, terracotta and cocoa on the other. The palette is the campaign — milk on the left of the frame, bronze on the right — so the two-step idea reads before the words do.

Imagery

Product on ceramic, one soft source.

No model in the paid frames — the story is told in materials: glazed cream dishes, a terracotta bowl, poured milk, a swipe of bronze, sand and a towel for the summer beat. Daylight from one side keeps the shadows long and the surfaces honest, and it leaves the faces to the creators.

Layout

Line up top, product low, numbers in the margin.

Wordmark top-left, the line in the upper third clear of the interface, product weighted into the lower half. "01." and "02." sit in the side margin so each frame states its place in the routine, and the speculative disclaimer is pinned to the foot of every asset.

Copy

Three words, one order of operations.

"Milk, then bronze." is the whole campaign, with "Two steps. One finished glow." underneath as the promise. Every step frame carries a two-word instruction — glow first, warm it up — so a creator can read the asset and know the beat to film.

Format

9:16 discovers, 4:5 banks.

The verticals are built to be watched and stitched — key visual first, then one frame per step. The 4:5 cuts are built to be saved: both products in one lockup for the feed, and a lifestyle still for the seasonal push. Same system, resized on purpose, not exported once for both.
